\section{Methods}

We evaluate short-form factual QA generations across SimpleQA, TriviaQA,
NQ-Open, PopQA, and TruthfulQA. For each answer, hidden states are saved at
multiple token positions: the final prompt token, first answer token, last answer
token, and mean answer representation.

Linear probes are trained to predict correctness from residual-stream states.
Baselines include token log-probability, entropy, margin, and verbalized
confidence. Causal experiments use activation patching: hidden states from a
correct run are inserted into an incorrect run, and the change in answer-token
logit difference is measured.
