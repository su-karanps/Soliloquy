\section{Discussion}

The experiments support three conclusions. First, language models contain an
internal correctness signal that is not equivalent to outward confidence.
Second, causal control is concentrated in late-layer states, especially MLP
outputs. Third, linear probe directions should be interpreted carefully: a
direction can be predictive without being an effective steering vector.

Future work should test richer multi-dimensional interventions, broader model
families, and less artificial prompting distributions than forced-answer QA.
